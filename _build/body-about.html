Our Story

A clinic built around how you feel

Dr. Elena Vasquez opened Lumina with one belief: clinical excellence means nothing if patients are scared to walk through the door. Twenty-four years later, that idea still shapes every room, every protocol and every conversation in our clinic.

Our Vision

To make premium dental care calm, transparent and accessible for every family in the city.

Our Mission

Deliver evidence-based treatment with modern technology and zero judgement — ever.

“Treat the person, then the tooth.”

That sentence is printed on every operatory door in our clinic — and it's the first thing every new team member learns.

What We Stand For

Four values, non-negotiable

They guide how we hire, how we treat, and how we charge.

Gentleness First

Needle-free anesthesia, sedation options and a team trained in anxiety-aware care.

Honest Advice

We only recommend treatment you actually need — and we'll tell you when you don't.

Digital Precision

3D CBCT scans, intraoral cameras and CAD/CAM crowns for predictable outcomes.

Whole Family

From first baby teeth to full-mouth implants — one clinic for every generation.

Milestones

Two decades of steady growth

From a two-chair room on Riverside Avenue to a 12-chair digital smile studio.

2002
The first two chairs

Dr. Elena Vasquez opens a small family practice with one hygienist and a promise of unhurried appointments.

2009
Implant centre opens

We introduce guided implant surgery and become one of the first clinics in the region with an in-house CBCT.

2015
Purpose-built studio

Moving into our Riverside location: 12 operatories, a kid zone, private consultation suites and heated dental chairs.

2020
Same-day digital lab

In-house CAD/CAM milling means crowns, veneers and onlays designed, milled and fitted in a single visit.

2026
12,000 smiles later

A team of 18 specialists, an award-winning clinic and a 99% recommendation rate — still counting.
